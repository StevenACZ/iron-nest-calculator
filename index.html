About this Iron Nest calculator

Free, unofficial ballistic calculator for IRON NEST: Heavy Turret Simulator. Enter azimuth, distance and powder charges and get the exact gun elevation using the in-game formula (12° per km per charge, 5 km of range per charge, 30 km max). Shot cards for both guns, fully keyboard-driven, and everything stays in your browser.

How to use it

  1. Enter the azimuth to the target and the distance in kilometers read from the map.
  2. Pick the powder charges or leave AUTO: the tool suggests the lowest charge that reaches the target.
  3. Read the required elevation and log the shot on gun 1 or gun 2. Cards can be dragged between guns and each volley is kept in the history.

The in-game formula

Elevation in degrees equals the distance in meters times 0.012 divided by the number of charges, capped at 60°. Each charge adds 5 km of reach: one charge covers 5 km and six charges cover 30 km. Anything beyond 30 km is out of range. The round type (HE, AP, DRIL, STAR, EMPT) is recorded on the shot card and does not change the elevation.

Keyboard

Enter advances and logs · Tab moves through all controls · Q/E or ←/→ change the charge · Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes the last shot · R resets and saves the volley · C switches the target gun.

FAQ

How does the Iron Nest ballistic calculator work?
It uses the in-game ballistic model: elevation in degrees equals distance in meters times 0.012 divided by the number of powder charges, capped at 60 degrees. Each charge extends maximum range by 5 km, up to 30 km with six charges. The tool always suggests the lowest charge that reaches the target.
Is this an official IRON NEST tool?
No. This is an unofficial, free, fan-made companion tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the developers of IRON NEST: Heavy Turret Simulator.
Is it free and does it store my data?
Yes, it is completely free with no accounts and no tracking. Shot cards and volley history are stored locally in your browser only.
Is it available in Spanish?
Yes. The calculator is bilingual: it detects your browser language and you can switch between Spanish and English with one click.

Sobre esta calculadora de Iron Nest

Calculadora balística gratuita y no oficial para IRON NEST: Heavy Turret Simulator. Introduce azimut, distancia y cargas de pólvora y obtén la elevación exacta del cañón con la fórmula del juego (12° por km por carga, 5 km de alcance por carga, 30 km máximo). Tarjetas de disparo para ambos cañones, todo por teclado, y todo se queda en tu navegador.

Cómo se usa

  1. Introduce el azimut hacia el objetivo y la distancia en kilómetros leída en el mapa.
  2. Elige las cargas de pólvora o deja AUTO: la herramienta sugiere la carga más baja que llega al objetivo.
  3. Lee la elevación requerida y registra el disparo en el cañón 1 o 2. Las tarjetas se arrastran entre cañones y cada tanda queda en el historial.

La fórmula del juego

La elevación en grados es la distancia en metros por 0.012 entre el número de cargas, con un máximo de 60°. Cada carga añade 5 km de alcance: una carga cubre 5 km y seis cargas cubren 30 km. Más allá de 30 km el objetivo queda fuera de alcance. El tipo de obús (HE, AP, DRIL, STAR, EMPT) se anota en la tarjeta y no cambia la elevación.
